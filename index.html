WHY IAF MULTITOOLS EXISTS

Why pay for a cloud server when your own computer can do the job?

A lot of modern tools send relatively simple workloads to remote servers. That can mean subscriptions, credits, generation limits, queues, and unnecessary data transfers.

IAF MultiTools is our attempt to solve that problem by bringing those workloads closer to the user whenever practical. Instead of automatically sending everything to the cloud, we look for ways to let your own hardware handle the work.

The Idea

Why we prefer local processing when possible.

Cloud isn't always necessary.

Cloud infrastructure is useful, but not every task needs a remote server to function.

If a workload can reasonably run on your own hardware, why not let your hardware do it?

No artificial limits.

Local processing doesn't need a credit counter for every generation. Your practical limit is generally the hardware, storage, and time available on your own device.

More control.

Keeping processing closer to the user can reduce unnecessary uploads, external dependencies, and recurring service costs.

Important Information

Local-first does not necessarily mean completely offline. Some tools may require an internet connection for downloading models, loading assets, checking updates, or using optional online functionality.

Processing behavior can differ between tools. Each individual tool should explain whether your data stays on your device or whether an external service is involved.

Performance depends on your hardware. CPU, GPU, RAM, browser capabilities, storage speed, thermals, and the size of the workload can all affect processing time.
