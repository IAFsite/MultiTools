ABOUT IAF MULTITOOLS

Not everything needs a cloud server.

Modern software has made it incredibly easy to put almost everything in the cloud. Sometimes that's exactly what we need. Sometimes it isn't.

A simple piece of audio processing, a video conversion, a local generation task, or a small content workflow may not need a remote server, an account, a subscription, or a monthly pool of credits. IAF MultiTools exists to explore that alternative. We build small utilities that prioritize local processing whenever it makes practical sense.

The Philosophy

A few principles behind the project.

Cloud isn't bad.

Cloud infrastructure is incredibly useful. It makes powerful software accessible from almost anywhere.

The point isn't to eliminate the cloud. The point is to avoid using it when it isn't necessary.

Hardware matters.

Your computer is already capable of processing a surprising amount of work. MultiTools tries to make better use of that hardware.

Keep things useful.

No unnecessary dashboards. No giant enterprise workflow. Just tools that do the thing they were built to do.

Local First
