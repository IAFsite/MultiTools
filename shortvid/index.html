ABOUT SHORT VIDEO

Short-form content doesn't need to become a giant workflow.

Short videos can involve scripts, narration, subtitles, media processing, formatting, and other small tasks. Sometimes you don't need one massive platform that tries to handle everything.

IAF MultiTools treats these tasks as separate utilities. Open the tool you actually need, process the task, and continue with the rest of your workflow.

The Philosophy

A few principles behind the project.

Cloud isn't bad.

Cloud infrastructure is incredibly useful. It makes powerful software accessible from almost anywhere.

The point isn't to eliminate the cloud. The point is to avoid using it when it isn't necessary.

Hardware matters.

Your computer is already capable of processing a surprising amount of work. MultiTools tries to make better use of that hardware.

Keep things useful.

No unnecessary dashboards. No giant enterprise workflow. Just tools that do the thing they were built to do.
